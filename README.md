# From-Data-Strategy-to-Activation-Data-Con-101-Practice-Questions-and-a-Study-Guide
Salesforce Certified Data 360 Consultant is intended for consultants who design, configure, and implement Data 360 solutions for customer use cases. The exam spans business positioning, setup, ingestion, unification, analysis, and activation. A useful study plan follows that data journey rather than treating the six domains as unrelated product features.

## Exam introduction and overview

The Salesforce exam guide aligns to the Spring '26 release. It describes 60 multiple-choice questions, with up to five additional unscored questions, a 105-minute time limit, and a 70% passing score. The exam is proctored online or at a test center. The certification page lists no prerequisite. Check Salesforce's current guide before scheduling because exam details can change.

## Official exam domains

- **Solution Positioning — 14%.** Explain Data 360 terminology and business value, its role in generative and predictive AI, initial use cases, and data ethics and governance.
- **Data 360 Setup and Administration — 13%.** Configure permissions and organization settings, apply governance requirements, manage the development lifecycle, and diagnose common issues.
- **Data Source Connection and Ingestion — 18%.** Understand transformations, ingestion from varied sources, and collaboration methods including Zero-Copy.
- **Harmonization and Unification — 17%.** Model data and explain the purpose, process, and use cases of unification.
- **Data Enhancements, Sharing, and Analysis — 18%.** Build insights, reference Data 360 data from other systems, use reporting and dashboards, and apply predictive or generative AI capabilities to customer scenarios.
- **Data Activations and Utilization — 20%.** Manage segments, publish activations, act on data, and use Data 360 in Salesforce flows.

These official weights add to 100%. Activation is the largest section, but each earlier stage supplies data or controls needed for later work.

## Study the end-to-end workflow

Choose a customer scenario and write down its business outcome. Identify the data sources, ingestion method, governance constraints, and required transformations. Model the data and explain how unification would serve the use case. Then decide what insight or segment is needed and what action the activation should enable.

This exercise links technical decisions to customer requirements. It also helps separate similar sounding concepts: bringing data in, harmonizing it, deriving insight, and using the result are different steps.

## Using Data-Con-101 Practice Questions

CertQuestionsBank [Data-Con-101 practice questions](https://www.certquestionsbank.com/Data-Con-101.html) can support a structured review after each domain. Read the explanations, identify why a choice fits the scenario, and record the objective behind each missed question. Use mixed sets after you can explain the complete workflow. These questions are independent study material, not official Salesforce exam items.

## Six preparation tips

1. Use the official Salesforce guide as the scope reference.
2. Map each practice question to one of the six domains.
3. Practice permissions and governance alongside ingestion design.
4. Explain how modeling and unification affect downstream analysis.
5. Trace a segment through activation and a Salesforce flow.
6. Recheck current exam and maintenance requirements before registration.

## Why the credential matters

Data 360 consultants need to connect customer goals with platform configuration and usable outcomes. This credential addresses that breadth. Review the Salesforce Certified Data 360 Consultant Exam Guide, build practical examples, and use CertQuestionsBank explanations to identify where further study is needed.
